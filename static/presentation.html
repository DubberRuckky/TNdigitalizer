SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026 01 / PROBLEM

Legacy land records are difficult to digitize and verify

The challenge is not simply scanning paper — it is reliably understanding, validating and locating what the record represents.

DEGRADED RECORDS

Old registers may be faded, physically damaged, brittle or incomplete with missing margins.

HANDWRITTEN / REGIONAL

Complex regional scripts (Tamil, Marathi, Hindi) and varied cursive handwriting sharply reduce OCR accuracy.

MANUAL VERIFICATION

Revenue officers spend overwhelming hours manually comparing physical ledgers and re-typing records.

MISSING SPATIAL LINK

Textual records (Chitta/Patta) and cadastral field maps (FMB) remain disconnected, creating fraud vectors.

Result: slow processing • uncertain data • difficult public access • higher verification effort 2
SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026 02 / PROPOSED SOLUTION

AI-assisted Patta digitization with human control

A single unified workflow that dynamically adapts to the age and condition of the document.

1

OFFICIAL SCAN

Only authorized documentation officers scan legacy physical records using local edge equipment.

2

LOCAL AI

A local AI model processes the scan and produces a structured digital document in real-time.

3

VALIDATE

AI checks fields against historical statutory records and flags missing or uncertain content.

4

HUMAN REVIEW

Names, places, areas and ambiguous legal facts remain strictly under officer control.

Guiding Principle: AI handles pattern recognition and repetitive work — humans retain authority over legal identity and ambiguous facts. 3
SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026 03 / ADAPTIVE PROCESSING

Different records need different levels of AI assistance

The system changes its workflow based on document age and extraction confidence.

OLDER PATTA • 50+ YEARS Human-Scanned
Authorized Documentation Officer: Performs physical scan of delicate parchment registers.
Local AI Engine: Reads, reconstructs document structure and searches related legal text for missing pieces.
Human Officer Decision: Strictly verifies names • places • areas • ambiguous legal facts.
RECENT PATTA AI-First
AI-First Processing: AI performs most extraction, structuring, field mapping and consistency checks automatically.
Human Exception Handling: Only uncertain, conflicting or legally ambiguous fields are routed to an officer.
High Throughput: Accelerates processing of modern computerized deeds by >90%.
Design Goal: Automate routine records without removing vital human oversight. 4
SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026 04 / DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE

When a record is incomplete, AI tries to reconstruct context

The model does not invent missing legal facts. It searches for supporting evidence and sends unresolved ambiguity to a human.

PATTA SCAN

Missing / unclear / torn degraded text

AI SEARCH

Related records, gazettes, survey acts

MATCH

Candidate legal text + confidence score

HUMAN

Resolve ambiguity or reject suggestion

CRITICAL LEGAL SAFEGUARD:

Names, places and land areas are never hallucinated. When OCR confidence is low or text is missing, these fields are explicitly handed over to the documentation officer for sovereign legal verification.

SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026 05 / SECURITY & TRACEABILITY

A tamper-evident identity for every processed Patta

A unique 30-character identifier using non-confusing alphanumeric symbols is stamped onto the document and stored.

DOCUMENT SECURITY ID
7KQ-4X9-M2R-8TZ-P6C-3NW-H5D-9VA-2LM

Base-30 charset (no 0, 1, I, O) • Cryptographically bound to image SHA-256 digest

STAMP: Identifier is physically applied to the processed Patta.
STORE: Identifier is stored with the digital record and audit metadata.
TRACE: Connects physical document, scan and digital database.
LEGAL ACCESS: Civil courts can subpoena and verify immutable records.
Security Goal: Make unauthorized substitution or silent alteration immediately detectable and legally traceable. 6
